Microsoft announced the private preview of Frontier Tuning, a new approach to customizing AI behavior using reinforcement learning inside an organization's compliance boundary with its own data, processes, and conventions. Unlike traditional fine-tuning, Frontier Tuning adapts AI models to reflect the specific workflows and norms of a business without sending data outside the tenant. The feature is aimed at enterprise developers who need AI that behaves consistently with internal standards.
